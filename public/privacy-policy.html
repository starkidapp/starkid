Privacy Policy

StarBo · Effective date: October 20, 2026

StarBo ("we", "us") is a family task and reward app. Parents create an account and add their children, assign tasks, and award stars. This policy explains what we collect and how we use it.

1. Information we collect

  • Parent account: name, email address and password. Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication and are never visible to us.
  • Information parents enter about children: a child's name or nickname, chosen avatar, a 4-digit PIN, tasks, completed tasks, stars, bonuses and penalties.
  • Parent access code: a 4-digit code that protects the parent area.

We do not use advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs, and we do not access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or location.

2. How we use information

Only to provide the app: to sign you in, show your family's tasks and stars, and sync your data across your devices.

3. Children's privacy

StarBo is intended to be set up and managed by a parent or guardian. Children do not create accounts and do not provide personal information directly; all child information is entered and controlled by the parent. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children without parental involvement, and we do not show ads to anyone.

4. Storage and third parties

Data is stored with Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore), which processes data on our behalf under Google's privacy terms. Data is encrypted in transit. We do not sell or share personal information with anyone else, except where required by law.

5. Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account is active. You can delete your account and all associated data (including all children's profiles, tasks and stars) at any time:

  • In the app: Parent Profile → Delete Account. Deletion is immediate.
  • Or by email — see Delete your account.

6. Your rights

You can view and correct your information in the app at any time, and request a copy or deletion of your data by contacting us.

7. Changes

If we change this policy we will update this page and the effective date above.
