Delete your StarBo account

You can permanently delete your StarBo account and all of its data at any time.

Option 1: In the app (immediate)

  1. Open StarBo and sign in as the parent.
  2. Go to Parent Profile.
  3. Tap Delete Account, enter your password and confirm.

Option 2: By email

If you can no longer access the app, email starkid.app@gmail.com from the email address on your account with the subject "Delete my StarBo account". We will delete your account within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

Your parent account (name, email, login), your parent access code, and all children's profiles, PINs, tasks, completions, stars, bonuses and penalties. Nothing is kept after deletion.
